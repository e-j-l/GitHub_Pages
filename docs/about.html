About Me!

Personal Life

I grew up near this family farm. I never thought about this farm really. I only knew we got our christmas tree from them every year. When I turned 16 I knew I wanted to start making my own money. So, I decided I was going to ask the farm if they needed any help. If you told me it would lead to the most important memories and learning moments in my life I would have never believed you.

After 6 years of working on the farm, I have grown from the little kid I used to be to the man graduating next year. I love the farm and farming is something I will always carry with me. The memories skill, but most importantly the person I am today comes from everything I learned and grew through during my years working on the farm.

Professional Life

I am a junior mechanical engineering student. I love taking on projects that push me to the limits of what I am capable of. I enjoy both mechanical design, but also the electrical and programing side of projects.

At Quinnipiac University I am a part of the university's BAJA SAE team as the driver safety and restraint designer. As a mechanical engineer with a minor in physics and computer science, I spend most of my time either working on personal machine/circuits projects or designing, testing and manufacturing parts for the BAJA car.
